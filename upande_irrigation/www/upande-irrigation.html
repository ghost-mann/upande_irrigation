Guide

Filters in the sidebar (farm, period, dates) apply to every view. Records live in the desk under Upande Irrigation.

Overview

The morning check. KPI tiles for this week's water, energy and plan, and a Needs attention list: deficits, stale sensors, overrides left on, missing weather readings. Each alert links to where you fix it.

Irrigation plan

Each block keeps a daily water balance: crop use from the pan reading, minus the rain that stays in the root zone and the irrigation actually recorded. A block is due once it has used its irrigation allowance (RAW, about half of what its root zone holds). Today is the run sheet: tick each cycle Start, Done, Partial or Skip — that is what the balance counts. Week shows when each shift comes due; Blocks each block's soil water; Budget needed vs recorded vs metered water. Soil, tree age and emitters come from each Irrigation Block Profile.

Weather

Daily Weather Reading: pan evaporation, rain, min/mean/max temperature (the shaded band is the day's range). Log today's reading in the form at the top. Irrigation sections summarises the shift mapping; Blocks shows the latest irrometer tension per block.

Compare

The same period across years, so you can see whether this season is drier or wetter than the last.

IoT Sensors

All sensors shows every device: its latest value, Live or Stale (silent for more than 6 h), battery, RSSI and SNR. Click a card to chart that sensor below. Recent readings lists the newest raw rows. Use the period in the sidebar to look further back.

Water & Energy

Where the water went: pumping into the dam, transfers, and what each section used, with energy per m³. The meter tables show each reading's previous and new values; enter readings on Water Meter Reading and Electricity Meter Reading.

Field Map

Valves in 3D over the block boundaries on Esri imagery; switch between Satellite, Hybrid and Streets. Open valves pulse green. Click a valve to see its state and set Auto / On / Off right there. The map follows the farm filter.

Irrigation Now

Which cycles on today's run sheet are running, how long they have left, and what comes next on each section.

Valve Control

Every valve with its effective state: the schedule, unless an operator override says otherwise. Override one valve, a whole group (On all / Off all / Auto all), or everything in view with Close all, Open all and Reset all to Auto. Each bulk action asks you to confirm the count. Show on map jumps to a valve's location.
